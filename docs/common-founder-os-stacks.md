# Common Founder OS Stacks

Use these stacks when one repo is not enough but the full suite would be too much.

| Stack | Who should use it | Repos included | First repo to open | First output to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founder-led GTM stack | Founders finding accounts, preparing calls, learning from conversations, and rescuing deals. | `ai-gtm-command-center`, `founder-led-sales-call-os`, `founder-outreach-tracker` | `ai-gtm-command-center` | Account briefs or call prep output |
| Revenue diagnosis stack | Founders and RevOps operators trying to understand why pipeline is not converting. | `founder-led-sales-call-os`, `founder-os-revenue-engine`, `startup-metrics-playbook` | `founder-os-revenue-engine` | Leakage diagnosis or weekly GTM action list |
| Post-sale activation stack | Founders closing customers but seeing messy onboarding, unclear handoffs, slow activation, or weak post-activation follow-through. | `founder-customer-onboarding-os`, `founder-retention-expansion-os`, `founder-weekly-operating-review-agent` | `founder-customer-onboarding-os` | Founder onboarding memo |
| Retention and expansion stack | Founders protecting renewals, finding expansion, and turning customer success into proof. | `founder-retention-expansion-os`, `founder-weekly-operating-review-agent`, `board-pack-investor-update-agent` | `founder-retention-expansion-os` | Founder retention memo |
| Product roadmap stack | Founders deciding what customer signals should become roadmap work, validation, non-product fixes, deferrals, or rejects. | `founder-led-sales-call-os`, `founder-customer-onboarding-os`, `founder-retention-expansion-os`, `founder-product-feedback-roadmap-os` | `founder-product-feedback-roadmap-os` | Founder roadmap memo |
| AI leverage stack | Founders who want AI leverage without random experimentation. | `founder-ai-workflow-roi-os`, `founder-weekly-operating-review-agent` | `founder-ai-workflow-roi-os` | Founder AI ROI memo |
| Hiring and talent stack | Founders deciding which roles to hire, delay, automate, outsource, or redefine, and which candidates should advance. | `founder-ai-workflow-roi-os`, `founder-hiring-talent-pipeline-os`, `founder-weekly-operating-review-agent` | `founder-hiring-talent-pipeline-os` | Founder hiring memo |
| RevOps build stack | Teams needing CRM stages, handoffs, reporting, and revenue operating discipline. | `revops-infrastructure-playbook`, `founder-os-revenue-engine`, `startup-metrics-playbook` | `revops-infrastructure-playbook` | Field map or pipeline stage design |
| Board and investor stack | Founders turning internal metrics into investor-ready narrative. | `founder-weekly-operating-review-agent`, `board-pack-investor-update-agent`, `edtech-saas-financial-model` | `founder-weekly-operating-review-agent` | Weekly operating review |
| Full Founder OS stack | Founders who want the full operating lifecycle from GTM to investor narrative. | `founder-os`, `founder-ai-workflow-roi-os`, `founder-hiring-talent-pipeline-os`, `ai-gtm-command-center`, `founder-led-sales-call-os`, `founder-os-revenue-engine`, `founder-customer-onboarding-os`, `founder-retention-expansion-os`, `founder-product-feedback-roadmap-os`, `founder-weekly-operating-review-agent`, `board-pack-investor-update-agent` | `founder-os` | Founder OS map |

## How to choose

- Use one repo when the operating problem is narrow.
- Use 2 to 4 repos when outputs from one workflow naturally feed the next.
- Use the full stack only when you are building a broader operating system.
- Keep private company, customer, prospect, employee, investor, and financial data out of public forks.
