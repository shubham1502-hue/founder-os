# Sample Founder Operating Cadence

## Monday

- Review weekly metrics.
- Identify the one growth constraint and one operating constraint.
- Assign owners.

## Tuesday

- Review target accounts and founder outreach.
- Decide which accounts deserve founder time.

## Wednesday

- Inspect CRM hygiene, stuck deals, handoffs, and reporting gaps.

## Thursday

- Draft investor-safe summary.
- Capture risks, asks, and decisions.

## Friday

- Close the weekly review.
- Update follow-up tracker.
- Decide what changes next week.
