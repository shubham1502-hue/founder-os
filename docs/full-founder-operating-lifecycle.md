# Full Founder Operating Lifecycle

Founder OS can be used as one repo at a time or as a connected operating lifecycle. The integrations are operating handoffs, not hard technical dependencies.

## Lifecycle

1. Decide AI leverage and operating priorities.
2. Decide which roles need hiring, delay, automation, outsourcing, or sharper definition.
3. Build RevOps and metrics foundation.
4. Research target accounts.
5. Prepare and run founder-led calls.
6. Learn from calls.
7. Diagnose revenue leakage.
8. Move closed-won customers into onboarding.
9. Track activation.
10. Monitor retention, renewals, expansion, and customer proof after activation.
11. Turn customer signals into roadmap decisions.
12. Run weekly operating reviews.
13. Convert operating signal into board or investor narrative.

## Practical handoff

Use outputs as handoff artifacts:

- Account briefs and outreach drafts become sales call prep.
- Hire-vs-automate decisions and team bottlenecks become role priority and candidate pipeline inputs.
- Sales call notes become objection banks and deal rescue queues.
- Deal risks become revenue leakage signals.
- Close-won accounts become onboarding tracker rows.
- Activated accounts become retention and expansion tracker rows.
- Sales objections, onboarding blockers, support issues, churn drivers, expansion asks, and metrics become product feedback tracker rows.
- Product feedback outputs become build, validate, non-product fix, defer, or reject decisions.
- Retention risks, expansion opportunities, and churn drivers become weekly operating review topics.
- Product roadmap decisions and product risks become weekly operating review topics.
- Hiring priorities, candidate decisions, offer risks, and team-capacity gaps become weekly operating review topics.
- Weekly operating signal becomes board or investor narrative.

You can stop at any step. The lifecycle is a map, not a requirement.
